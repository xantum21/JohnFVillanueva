About me

I have never been very good at having just one interest.

I'm Filipino American, early thirties, based in Northern California, with family and history spread across the U.S., Australia, and the Philippines. That mix is probably the single most useful thing about how I see the world — I grew up assuming that home can be more than one place and that most things are worth being curious about.

I'm energetic and expressive in a way that is either charming or a lot, depending on the day. I talk with my hands. I get genuinely excited about things that do not warrant it. I remember what people tell me, and I will bring it up three weeks later like it's a completely normal thing to have retained.

Work-wise: healthcare, plus a business background I still use, plus an ongoing habit of building small projects for the fun of it. I take my career seriously — but I'd rather be interesting at dinner than impressive on paper, and the professional details live on the rest of this site if you're curious.

Mostly I'm someone with a full life who would like to share it. Not fill a gap — share it.

What I'm looking for

Kind, funny, and going somewhere of your own.

The qualities I keep coming back to are pretty simple: kindness, reliability, a sense of humor, and enough emotional steadiness that a disagreement stays a conversation. I'd rather be with someone who says what they mean than someone who is easy to guess.

I'm drawn to people who have something they care about — a career, a craft, a cause, a project. It doesn't need to look like mine or be impressive to anyone else. I just find direction attractive, and I like being around someone whose goals I can cheer for.

We don't have to share every interest. Honestly, I'd rather you had a few of your own that I get to learn about. What matters more is being curious about each other and actually enjoying the time we spend together.

And affection matters to me. I'm a warm, expressive, physically affectionate person, and I'd like to be with someone who's comfortable being that way back.
