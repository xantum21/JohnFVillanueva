How I work

The setting changes. These habits don’t.

Bedside care, banking, class projects, and browser games are very different. I still approach each one in roughly the same way.

01

Pay attention

Notice what is changing, what is missing, and what the person in front of me actually needs.

02

Make a plan

Break the problem into steps and keep the important parts visible.

03

Explain it clearly

Use plain language, ask better questions, and make sure everyone understands the next step.

04

Revise without drama

Try it, see what fails, fix it, and keep moving.
