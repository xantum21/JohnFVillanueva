# V27 Vibe-First Order Review

This version prioritizes how the playlist *feels* in continuous playback:
production style, perceived energy, instrumentation, genre neighborhood,
and emotional arc. Literal BPM was not used as a hard sorting rule.

Changes from V26:
- Part I: moved “From the Start” to #2 so the chapter gets its energetic indie-rock lift early, then settles into acoustic/theatrical/soulful material.
- Part III: moved Novelbright’s “愛とか恋とか” immediately before “I GUESS I'M IN LOVE” to make the back half descend more naturally from full-band J-pop into confessional ballads.
- Part IV: placed “I Love You Always Forever” before “Still Falling For You,” using Betty Who as the bright synth-pop peak and Ellie Goulding as the cinematic resolution.
- Part VI: restructured the wedding/forever sequence into intimate acoustic → cinematic wedding climax → energetic Higedan lift → quiet lifetime epilogue.
- Parts II and V were left unchanged.
- “LOVE YOU FOR LIFE.” remains beside the R&B/electronic/pop cluster because sonic fit takes priority over perfectly literal lyric chronology inside a chapter.
