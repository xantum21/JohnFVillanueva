# Playlist order review — V14

This is the preliminary narrative order currently wired into the site. Content copy can be revised later without changing the player architecture.

## Part I — The Crush
Before anything is certain: noticing them, freezing up around them, wondering if the feeling is mutual, and finally risking the question.

1. **this is what having a massive crush on someone feels like** — Clinton Kane
2. **Superman** — Joe Brooks
3. **From the Start** — Good Kid
4. **How Will I Know** — Sam Smith
5. **Like Me** — AJ Rafael

Transition: *Eventually, wondering if there is a chance becomes noticing that maybe there is.*

## Part II — Wait… You Like Me Too?
The first real shift: a hello that feels different, trust beginning to form, hands finding each other, and the first kiss becoming possible.

6. **Everything Has Changed** — Taylor Swift & Ed Sheeran

## High-impact moves
- **Make You Mine** is no longer treated as an early mutual-attraction song because its own lyric arc eventually reaches a proposal and “I do.”
- **Never Seen Anything “Quite Like You”** now sits in the wedding chapter because the song looks back across ordinary appearances/prom and explicitly arrives at seeing the partner “in white.”
- **Luckiest Man Alive** is now in the wedding sequence because it is explicitly an aisle/vows/wife song.
- **Better Half of Me**, **Just You and I**, and **Palagi** now sit in Building a Life because their supplied lyrics emphasize distance, hard seasons, home, repeated choosing, and long-horizon partnership.
