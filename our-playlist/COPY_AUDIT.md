# V20 Copy Audit

This pass removes or rewrites visible copy that felt explanatory, generic, or overly polished. The goal is to let the song order carry the meaning and keep the site voice short, personal, and a little self-aware.

## Main decisions

- Removed all six chapter-description paragraphs. The chapter title, short kicker, and songs now do the work.
- Rewrote chapter transitions as short thoughts rather than summaries.
- Replaced the tech-heavy player explainer with one sentence.
- Reworked the hero subtitle/note so they sound like something a person might actually say.
- Reworked the finale to be lighter and less storybook-like.
- Simplified the password modal.
- Removed technical text from the bottom of populated lyric letters.
- Hid the visible generated theme-name labels while preserving each song’s stationery visuals.
- Replaced “End of Part X” copy with a simple heart divider.
