    HISTORICAL NOTES

    WHAT HAPPENED

    In the last days of November 1574 the corsair Limahong (Lin Feng) sailed into Manila Bay with a large fleet and struck the three-year-old Spanish settlement twice within days. His force was a maritime coalition that included Chinese and Japanese fighters.

    Manila was then a town of timber, bamboo and nipa thatch behind a wooden palisade. The stone walls of Intramuros were built in later decades, so you will not find them here.

    The defenders, Spaniards together with local and other allies, held the town and the fleet withdrew north.

    WHAT IS INVENTED

    Mateo, Datu and Diego are fictional. So are the bosses, the martial-arts mercenary, Datu's stone yo-yo, and every supernatural event. No nationality here is a villain: these are individual raiders in one fleet.

    THE SPIRITS

    The Philippines holds many peoples and many bodies of lore. The powers sold between waves borrow from different traditions and are not one shared mythology.
