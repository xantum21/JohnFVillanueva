## Edits made

- Responsive crops
- Mild color and contrast grading
- Burgundy, gold, and navy angular framing to match the site identity
- Selective use of real personal photos on About, Life, Timeline, and the unlisted personal page
- Metadata-stripped, responsive WebP exports of the selected personal photos
- Original illustration for the Work hero and personal-page heritage card
- Original cinematic anime portrait based on John's supplied photograph
- WebP conversion and compression

No stock or generated subject is represented as an actual patient, client, coworker, or endorsement of John Villanueva. Personal photographs are presented as personal context, never as healthcare documentation.
